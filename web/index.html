finrag

Ask about the latest 10-K of Apple, Microsoft, NVIDIA or JPMorgan Chase. Every answer cites its filing section, every figure is checked against the cited text, and a refusal says which step refused.

Source: SEC EDGAR. Three open-weight models, pinned by digest, served by Ollama on one GPU that sleeps when idle: the first question after a pause can take a minute or two, and switching model loads it first. GPT and Claude are hosted by their vendors: the question and the retrieved filing passages are sent to them. Cost shown is GPU time (open models) or tokens x list price (hosted). Not investment advice.
API: REST (POST /api/query, /api/agent) or GraphQL, with its explorer. Code: github.com/HadiJamalAhmad/finrag.
